\section{Strong points of the paper}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Clean and transparent architecture
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The paper clearly describes the architecture of the forecasting model, making it easy to understand.
    \end{itemize}
    \item Well communicated design choices
    \begin{itemize}
        \item The authors provide clear explanations for their design choices.
        \item The authors do not oversell their approach and algorithm.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}

\section{Weak points of the paper}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Model Choice
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Choosing AR(2) as the forecasting model is quite obviously not the best choice for the problem.
        \begin{itemize}
            \item No trend
            \item No seasonality
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
    \item Reported Evaluation results
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Fig. 2 shows how badly the model is performing.
        \item This is nice to see, in that the authors are not overselling their approach and algorithm; however, it should have been a tell to use a different approach.
        \item The huge error is also not discussed enough in the paper.
    \end{itemize}
\end{enumerate}
